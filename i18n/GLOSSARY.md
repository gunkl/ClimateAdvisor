# Climate Advisor translation glossary (Issue #1341) — DRAFT

Brand **Climate Advisor** is never translated. Entity names, `owner/repo`, `notify.xxx`, units (°F/°C, kW, min) are never translated.

Conventions
- Cell format: `translation (g)` where g = noun gender (m/f/n) for fr es de pt it pl uk ru ar. nl/ja/ko/hi(has m/f but not used in ICU select here)/zh/tr: no gender marked. Use ICU `select` on gender only where a sentence needs agreement.
- `(?)` = not confident; native check needed. Cells in section 2 (modes, actions) are HA core exact strings (see style notes); other cells are my choice unless noted.
- Rule of choice: HA wording where HA has a term; else the everyday homeowner word.
- Column order: fr es de nl ja pt(PT-PT) it pl uk ko hi ar ru zh-Hans tr.
