## Scope

- **Files:** `custom_components/climate_advisor/invariant_watchdog.py` (checks, `PromiseNumbers`, `PromiseConfig`, `PROMISES`, `run_promise_checks`), `custom_components/climate_advisor/promise_journal.py` (evidence records and the bounded `PromiseJournal`), `coordinator.py` (`_tick_household_promises`, `_evaluate_household_promises`, `_promise_observe_command`), `tools/sim_harness/promise_probe.py`, `generator.py`, `replay.py`, `trial_gate.py`, `world.py`.
- **Entry points:** `run_promise_checks(inp, cfg, scope=...)`; `PromiseJournal.window(now)`.
- Not covered: the original hard invariant `check_ac_whf_mutex` contract ([invariant-watchdog-brief.md](invariant-watchdog-brief.md)); the real-HA tier ([DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md#real-ha-tier-the-house-markers-revert-proof-snapshots-step-2-issue-1127)).

Ground-truth rule (brief Invariant 1) with one amendment dated 2026-10-29: only P4 may read CA's own override/grace events (`Episode`/`Mark`); every other promise reads readings and issued commands only. A promise never encodes the algorithm (no gates, flags or FSM states), so a new code path that breaks the household outcome is still caught.

### P1 and P4 judging details (Issues #1150, #1151)

- **P1 lookback stops at same-side equipment (#1150).** A flip is opposite, then idle, then same-side, with no same-side run in between. Scanning back from a heat start, the first `heating` reading ends the search with no hit (the mirror for a cool start). A furnace restart after an earlier in-family heat run is a normal cycle, not a flip.
- **P1 excuse anchor (#1150, owner sign-off pending).** The 30 min occupant excuse is measured to the equipment start. `P1_EXCUSE_ANCHOR_ON_ARMING_COMMAND` (default `False`) additionally measures it to the first CA `set_hvac_mode` into the same family within 30 min before the start (the occupant spoke, CA armed in response, the equipment lagged). It widens the excuse (design Invariant 2), so it stays off until the owner approves; flipping it is a one-line change.
- **P4 reports one hit per reversal instant (#1151).** A `set_hvac_mode` and a `set_temperature` in the same tick are one reversal: dedupe by (episode, ts); the hit key is `override_respected:<episode>:<ts>` with no service. Wording: "... {N} min before your manual change was due to end." (N rounded half up) when grace remained, otherwise "... while your manual change was still in force."

### P7 Away and Vacation (Issues #1411/#1412)

**Occupant first:** on a normal Away day the house sat at 64°F, inside its Away band (60-80°F), and P7 said "The house stayed 3.0°F outside your comfort range for 50 minutes while Climate Advisor was in control" (goldens `tou_away_heat_bank_home_return_1404` and `..._surfaces_1404`; the same check runs log-only at home). P7 protects the comfort of the people at home; while Climate Advisor holds the house for Away or Vacation nobody is home, so those readings are not judged (this exclusion predates the fix; only how Away is known changed).

- **Away is read from the reading.** `Reading.occupancy` is the occupancy mode Climate Advisor acts on at that instant (`AutomationEngine._occupancy_mode`; production `coordinator._promise_occupancy()`, the probe `FakeHassPromiseSource._occupancy()`, compared by `tests/test_promise_adapter_parity.py`). `away` and `vacation` are unoccupied; `home` and `guest` are judged. A change of occupancy stores a reading at once. Cause 1 (#1411): P7 knew Away only from an `occupancy_setback` mark, and none is journaled when Away is set before the first classification (the handler has no band to write yet). The same gap hit every restart while Away (the journal starts empty).
- **State marks are carried past the window.** Cause 2 (#1412): with a setback journaled at 13:00:01, the window that ends at 16:05 no longer holds the mark but still holds the 13:05-15:40 Away readings, so the timeline started the window as "home". `PromiseJournal` now keeps, per state family (`STATE_MARK_FAMILIES`: the occupancy marks, and nat-vent start/exit marks), the newest mark that left the window (aged out, or evicted by the 128-mark cap) and hands it back as `PromiseInput.carried_state_marks`. Only P7's away and nat-vent timelines read it; `marks` is unchanged for every other check. A restart clears it. The mark timeline is the fallback for a source whose readings carry no occupancy (`None`); a reading's own occupancy always wins over the marks.
- **What still fails:** a reading that says Home or Guest is judged as before, from the first reading after the change (an arrival without heat running for more than 45 min is a miss). Tests: `tests/test_p7_away_occupancy_1411.py` (each cause alone, end to end through the real coordinator, and the negative controls).

### P7 thermostat-deadband exception (Issue #1260)

Owner-approved wording: "P7: while Climate Advisor is in control, the house is not outside the comfort range for more than 45 min, except while the thermostat itself is in the matching mode, idle, set at or inside the band edge, and the room is within 2.5°F of both that edge and the thermostat's own setpoint (the thermostat's own deadband)."

Occupant view: the Activity log no longer says the house stayed outside your comfort range while your thermostat was only waiting inside its own deadband; it still does when the thermostat is off, set wrong, or the room is more than 2.5°F past the band edge or more than 2.5°F from the thermostat's own setpoint.

The exception applies only when ALL hold, read from ground-truth `Reading` fields only (`_p7_thermostat_deadband`):

1. The live mode matches the side: `heat`, `heat_cool` or `auto` below the floor; `cool`, `heat_cool` or `auto` above the ceiling.
2. `hvac_action` is `idle` (exactly; an unreported action is not idle).
3. The thermostat's own setpoint is known and at or inside the band edge: heat uses `setpoint_f` >= floor, cool uses `setpoint_f` <= ceiling, `heat_cool`/`auto` use the matching `setpoint_low_f` / `setpoint_high_f` (read from the thermostat's `target_temp_low` / `target_temp_high`). An unknown setpoint is NOT excused (strict default).
4. The room is no more than `P7_THERMOSTAT_DEADBAND_F` = 2.5°F past the un-slackened band edge (a fixed constant, not the learned swing; aggressive savings does not widen it).
5. The room is ALSO no more than `P7_THERMOSTAT_DEADBAND_F` = 2.5°F from that same setpoint on the side it is correcting: heat `setpoint - indoor <= 2.5`, cool `indoor - setpoint <= 2.5`, `heat_cool`/`auto` the matching low/high setpoint (owner-approved tightening, 2026-11-02). A thermostat idle while the room is far below its own setpoint (heat set to 74 with the room at 65.6) is not its deadband and still counts. This condition is deliberately TIGHTER than production's #1258 comfort tolerance, which measures from the band edge only.

Everything else keeps `P7_SLACK_F` = 1.0°F. **No clock reset:** an excused reading continues its run exactly like any other out-of-range reading, so a run is timed from when the room FIRST left the range; an excused reading just can never be the one that crosses 45 min. **Independent code:** P7 has its own short rule and never calls production's `within_thermostat_hysteresis`; `tests/test_promise_numbers.py` proves P7's mode set equals production's matching-mode set, `P7_THERMOSTAT_DEADBAND_F` >= `COMFORT_SWING_TOLERANCE_MAX_F` (no tighter than production), and the exception is never looser than that cap (the grid runs with the setpoint at the band edge, where condition 5 equals condition 4).

**Visible, apart from hits:** `excused_p7_comfort_kept` / `run_promise_excused` return `Excused` records (`excused_by="thermostat_deadband"`, minutes, `RunKey`, `intervals`); they are never `Hit`s. At home the coordinator adds them to `promise_stats[<promise>]["excused"][<excused_by>]` (`runs`, `minutes`, `last_ts`; `hits` untouched, persisted and in the diagnostic snapshot) via `PromiseJournal.excused_progress` + `bump_promise_excused`. **Long stretches (Issue #1262):** `Excused.intervals` are the excused `(start, end)` spans still in the 3 h evidence window; `PromiseJournal.excused_progress(key, intervals)` keeps a `counted_until` per series and adds only the part of the intervals after it, then advances it, so a 4 h, 12 h or 24 h continuous stretch is counted in full and `last_ts` keeps moving (it used to cap at 180 min, the window's size). A new run is still detected by `key.start > the open run's end`. `counted_until` lives in the journal beside its readings and is not persisted: after a restart the journal is empty, so the pre-restart minutes (kept in the persisted stats) are never recounted and the continuing stretch opens a new run. In tests `PromiseReport.excused` and the nightly summary's `excused_by_promise` carry the same counts beside `hits_by_promise`.

Tunable numbers live in `PromiseNumbers` (e.g. `P5_BLIND_CONTINUE_MIN=35`, `P6_MAX_FLIPS=2`, `P7_MAX_MIN=45`, `P10_MAX_SWITCHES=2`, `P10_WINDOW_MIN=180`); each is deliberately looser than production's own allowance and `tests/test_promise_numbers.py` walks the legal configuration grid to prove it.

### P10 heat_cool_settles (Issue #1368)

**Occupant first:** with the heat setpoint above the cool setpoint (comfort 74/72) the furnace and the AC took turns all day: 8-40 heat/cool switches per generated day, against 0-5 for a normal 68/74 band. Nothing reported it. P10 is the owner's thrashed-controls rule G2 ("it settles") for the heat/cool family: Climate Advisor must stop switching and reach a stable mode.

**Rule (`check_p10_heat_cool_settles`):** a miss when Climate Advisor commanded more than `P10_MAX_SWITCHES` = 2 heat/cool switches within `P10_WINDOW_MIN` = 180 min (the window edge counts). One hit per switch past the limit, keyed by its time.

- **A switch** is a Climate Advisor thermostat command (`set_hvac_mode`, or `set_temperature` carrying a mode) into `heat` or `cool` while the current family is the other one. Off, `heat_cool`, `auto` and fan-only do not change the family, so pausing through off and back is not a switch, and heat, off, cool is.
- **The current family** is the last one Climate Advisor commanded or the occupant chose. Occupant mode changes come from `occupant_actions()` (a reported mode change with no CA command nearby). So Climate Advisor following your change is not a switch, and your own presses are never counted (owner rules A1-A3). Climate Advisor overwriting your choice IS counted: one overwrite (A3) is one switch, far below the limit, but overwriting press after press is a thrash.
- **Ground truth only:** commands sent and modes reported. No engine flags or FSM states.
- **The window** equals the journal's 3 h evidence age (`JOURNAL_MAX_AGE`), so home and tests judge the same thing. After a restart the journal is empty and P10 sees nothing until new switches arrive.

**Calibration (generated days, the real engine; `P10_MAX_SWITCHES` forced to 0 to measure):** the largest number of CA-commanded switches inside any 3 h window, over 300 normal coordinator days (master seeds 101-106, 50 days each), was 0 on 72 days, 1 on 208, 2 on 19 and 3 on one day (105000004). The CI smoke seeds 101 and 102 never exceed 2. That one day is a real find, not noise: at 10:30 a pre-cool sent cool at 69.5 F, 30 min later the heat arming margin switched to heat at the same 69.5 F, and the AC came back at 12:35. The inverted 74/72 band on seeds 101000001, 101000007, 102000003 reached 3 in 3 h on warm days (high 88 F, 8-9 switches a day) and 4-6 on mild days (high 72 F, 14-40 a day); the same seeds at 68/74 never exceeded 1. "More than 2 in 3 h" is the only cut that catches every inverted day and no normal one. The separation on warm inverted days is one switch: a 3 h window cannot see more of a day that thrashes slowly. A longer window would need evidence kept past the journal age (not done).

**Relation to P6:** P6 `mode_flip_rate` counts flips the thermostat reported, more than 2 per hour. The inverted-band thrash never exceeds 2 per hour, so P6 stays silent on it. P10 watches the slower, sustained pattern and counts only what Climate Advisor commanded.

**Severity:** warning in tests and log at home, like every new promise (see [Promotion](#promotion-to-alert-policy)). Tests: `tests/test_promise_heat_cool_settles_1368.py`, including an end-to-end inverted-band day through the real engine that fails if P10 or the thrash disappears.

## Home behaviour and severity

`ClimateAdvisorCoordinator._tick_household_promises()` runs on a 5-minute heartbeat: record a reading, then `_evaluate_household_promises()` runs `run_promise_checks(..., scope="home")`. For each new hit (P2 is skipped; it keeps its existing alert path in `_run_invariant_watchdog`): WARNING `Household promise missed (log only)` (`(alert)` for P9), `bump_promise_stats`, an `invariant_violation` event (`severity="log"`, or `"alert"` for P9, which also sends one push notification per episode), and a state save. A check that raises is logged and skipped at home but re-raised in tests.

- **Occupant/operator view:** the AI Investigator shows these as "Noted, not alerted" lines (`ai_skills_context.py`); diagnostics show per-promise hit counts and last timestamps (`diagnostic_snapshot.py`, `promise_stats`, counts and timestamps only).
- **Dedup:** event promises use the journal's `seen_keys` LRU (256); long-running hits (P5b, P7) use `RunKey` so a sliding window does not re-report the same run.

## The journal

`PromiseJournal` is a pure leaf (no HA imports, no I/O), in-memory only, fed by production hooks and by test adapters. Retention `JOURNAL_MAX_AGE` 3 h; caps: 400 readings, 128 commands, 128 marks, 64 episode marks, 8 open episodes, 256 seen keys. A reading is stored when a discrete field changed, indoor moved 0.5 F, or 5 min passed. After a restart it is empty, so history-needing checks see no data and report nothing (a blind window, never a false alarm). Only `promise_stats` is persisted (capped to the promise keys, type-validated by `sanitize_promise_stats`). Commands are journaled through `AutomationEngine._command_observer` and, for the coordinator's own fan reconcile command, `_promise_observe_command` directly (Issue #1129: that command carries no Context by design; investigated, not a defect, locked by tests).

## P4 listed exceptions

Source: `invariant_watchdog.py` (`_COMMON_LISTED`, `_THERMOSTAT_LISTED`, `_FAN_OVERRIDE_LISTED`, `_p4_end_is_listed`).

| Ending | Thermostat override | Fan override | Fan-off grace |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grace_expired`, `superseded`, `restart` | yes | yes | yes |
| `user_cancel*` (dashboard End Grace) | yes | yes | yes |
| `adopted`, `adopted_matching_decision`, `self_resolved` | yes | yes (shared set) | no |
| `ac_whf_mutex`, `new_override_during_grace` | yes | yes | no |
| `occupancy_*` (away/vacation) | yes | yes | no |
| `bedtime`, `morning_wakeup` | **no** (the #1119 shape) | yes | no |
| `whf_safety_stop` (Issue #1144: the WHF safety enforcer ended a fan override) | no | yes | yes |
| Rule 2, newest action wins (Issue #1144): the occupant turned the fan on AFTER their thermostat change and Climate Advisor then turned HVAC off | yes, narrowly (see below) | no | no |
| reason containing `ceiling` (#134) | no | no | yes |
| `grace_expired_early` (Issue #1204: a carried thermostat override, see below, whose replacing grace ended before its own grace would have) | **no** | n/a | n/a |
| `stuck_grace_recovered` | not excused | not excused | not excused |

**Rule-2 ending (`_p4_rule2_suppression_ends`).** A thermostat override ended by the occupant's newer fan press is accepted only when (1) the fan-on is an OCCUPANT action (`occupant_actions`: a physical change with no Climate Advisor fan command nearby, so CA's own fan starts never count), (2) it is stamped after the override began (past the 10 s start slack), and (3) the episode's ending coincides with it and a Climate Advisor HVAC-off (`set_hvac_mode`/`set_temperature` to off) follows within `P4_RULE2_SUPPRESS_S` (60 s, looser than production's 30 s bound). A CA-initiated off with no occupant fan-on before it is still a miss (`tests/test_promise_checks.py::TestP4Rule2FanPressEndsThermostatOverride`). Evidence for the narrowing: generated seeds 1000072, 4000054, 5000008, 5000066 ended exactly this way.

**A fan press that does NOT end the thermostat override (Issue #1204, owner decision A2).** Occupant first: under `hvac_fan` you lower the setpoint, then switch the thermostat blower On a minute later. Climate Advisor keeps your setpoint (`_manual_override_active` stays True) until the blower's grace ends; P4 used to close the episode as `superseded_unreplaced` at the press and report "ended your manual thermostat change 14 min early" (generated seeds 102000023, 302000001, 302000008, 311000012, 311000015, 313000019). Now the engine says so: when a `fan_manual_override` grace replaces a grace while the thermostat change is still protected (`_manual_change_protected()`), its `grace_cancelled` carries `continues: True` (the #1205 contract; `_cancel_grace_intentionally(replaced_by=...)`). The journal keeps a thermostat episode open on a `superseded` cancel with `continues: True` (it is "carried") and protects it until max(own grace end, replacing fan grace end): a later manual fan grace start extends `grace_end_ts`, and the grace expiry closes it as `grace_expired`, or as the unlisted `grace_expired_early` if the replacing grace ended more than 2 s before that later end. Production clears the override before it announces the expiry (`_on_grace_expired()`: `override_cleared` reason `grace_expired`, then `grace_expired`), so the `override_cleared` branch is where a carried episode closes; the `grace_expired` branch applies the same rule to a stream without that clear (pinned against the real engine's event order by `TestJournalReadsTheEnginesOwnEventOrder`). A carried episode cleared for any other reason keeps that reason. A whole-house-fan press never reaches this (rule 2 clears the thermostat change before the grace starts, so the rule-2 ending above applies). A replacement the engine does not vouch for (no `continues`, an automation grace) still closes as `superseded_unreplaced`. `continues` on any other cancel reason never keeps a thermostat episode open. Tests: `tests/test_p4_fan_override_end_1204.py`, `tests_ha/test_p4_fan_override_end_1204.py`, `tests/test_sim_generator.py::test_1204_fan_press_and_rule2_stop_days_keep_override_respected`.

**The blower back to Auto does NOT end the thermostat override either (Issue #1281, the A2 mirror).**
- **Occupant first:** the A2 sequence, then you set the blower back to Auto before its grace ends (generated seeds 9003000097, 5008000215, 5010000029). The fan-off grace replaces the blower grace, and your setpoint stays protected (`_manual_override_active` stays True) until that fan-off grace expires. P4 used to close the episode as `superseded_unreplaced` at the blower-off and report "ended your manual thermostat change 5 min early", although Climate Advisor wrote nothing to the thermostat until the fan-off grace expired.
- **The engine side:** `_cancel_grace_intentionally()` now vouches for a `fan_off` replacement exactly as it does for `fan_manual_override`. On a `superseded` cancel while `_manual_change_protected()` holds, it sets `continues: True`. A fan-off with nothing protected still says `False`, and so does a replacement that is not a fan grace (`nat_vent_exit_resume`).
- **Why the old semantics were wrong (test-infrastructure rule 8):** they reported an ending that never happened, then stopped P4 guarding the setpoint for the rest of the pause.
- **What still fails:** a Climate Advisor setpoint write inside that time still hits. A fan-off pause that ends before the carried end (max of its own grace and the blower grace) is still `grace_expired_early`.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_p4_fan_override_end_1204.py` (`TestBlowerOffKeepsTheSetpoint`, which feeds the real engine's event stream to the journal, and `TestEngineVouchesForTheSetpoint`), and `tests/test_sim_generator.py` (`_P4_FIXED_1281_SEEDS`).

**Rule 2 stopping an OLDER fan override (Issue #1204, owner decision A3).** When the occupant's newer heat/cool stands a running whole-house fan down (`_stand_down_whf_for_override_conflict()`, all four callers), the fan override ends with the stop (`fan_override_cleared`, reason `whf_safety_stop`, a listed fan ending), so the stop is not a P4 miss (generated seed 303000022). The WHF safety enforcer's own rule-2 (mutex) stop ends it the same way (`_after_whf_safety_stop()`), including when the occupant's heat/cool is not yet pending (start-up coalescing). `nat_vent_manual_override_exit` is deliberately not a listed ending.

**Setpoint change while Off, and two changes inside five minutes (Issues #1131/#1133, retired and extended by #1325).** A setpoint-only change while the thermostat is Off (including under CA's door pause) is not an override at all (owner ruling 2026-11-04: a thermostat that is Off takes no setpoint), so it opens no episode; the earlier #1131 record against the pre-pause mode is gone. A temperature-only change while the whole-house fan owns HVAC is likewise no override. Every DISTINCT occupant change emits `override_detected` (the engine's 5-minute dedup suppresses only an identical repeat), so each opens its own episode and the newer one supersedes the older (`superseded`). **Cancel attribution (Issue #1325):** `derive_episodes` lets a `grace_cancelled` with `reason=superseded` (and not `continues`) close only an override episode (thermostat or fan) that was open before the cancelled grace started (the last occupant-override `grace_started`). The engine emits that cancel at the NEW change's confirm delay, after the new `override_detected`; the old rule attributed it to an episode the cancelled grace never protected, closing the newer change `superseded_unreplaced` with no grace end and leaving it unprotected. The newer episode now stays open and takes its grace end from the following `grace_started`. If CA cancels the new change's own grace early it still closes it and P4 fires; if no new grace ever starts, a CA write over the newer change is still caught. **Fan mirror (same rule):** a newer fan press while the fan runs (an RF remote timer/speed change) emits `fan_manual_override` BEFORE the supersede cancel of the old press's grace; it used to close at the instant it started (`superseded`, no grace end), so a CA fan-off during, say, a 4 h remote-timer run went unchecked. It now stays open with its own grace end (`tests/test_promise_journal.py::TestIssue1325FanMirrorRemoteTimerChange`, `tests_ha/test_second_change_p4_1325.py`). P4 itself is unchanged; the 10 seeds x 100 coordinator-day calibration is `override_respected` 30 hits before the #1131/#1133/#1146/#1147/#1148 fixes and 0 after, so `tests/test_sim_generator.py::_UNTRIAGED_ON_HEAD` no longer lists it.

Reversal: thermostat mode differing from the occupant's, or a setpoint differing by at least 2.0 F; fan: the opposite on/off. A command equal to the occupant's value is a re-affirm. A newer occupant action after the episode start excuses a later CA command. A restart empties the journal, so restart survival (#1006, #1045) belongs to the real-HA lifecycle tests.

## Test-side use

- `PromiseProbe` (`tools/sim_harness/promise_probe.py`) owns no promise logic: it polls a `PromiseSource`, records into `PromiseJournal`, runs `run_promise_checks(scope="test")`, and takes severity from the registry. Hard hits fail; warning hits are held by a ratchet baseline (`promise_baseline.json`, may only go down). Known hits on LOCKED goldens are recorded in `promise_waivers.json` (scenario, promise, reason, issue), never by editing a golden.
- Goldens are checked through the probe (`tests/test_goldens_promises.py`); the real-HA tier feeds it as well (`tests_ha/test_goldens_realha.py`).
- Generated days: `python tools/simulate.py --generate N [--seeds S ...] [--profile coordinator|engine|mixed]` runs N random households per master seed closed-loop against the real engine (toy house in `world.py`); `--generate-day DAY_SEED` replays a seed; `--replay-generated FILE [--shrink]` replays and shrinks a saved failure. `day_seed = master_seed * 1_000_000 + index`. `tools/sim_harness/trial_gate.py` measures whether generated days find a known regression unaided. The generator knows no promise.
- Mechanism evidence and the nightly waiver ledger (Issue #1153): every `ProbeHit` carries `mechanism`, a flat record with every field present and `null` where unknowable (`tools/sim_harness/mechanism.py`: cause, command service/target/mode/reason/origin, episode kind/source/grace seconds, configured grace, override/grace FSM flags, sensor-open, commit). Nightly seeds are fresh, so `--generate N --waiver-ledger tools/sim_harness/nightly_waivers.json --issue-state github` tolerates a known hard hit only through a ledger entry tied to an OPEN issue, with `added`/`expires` (max 45 days apart), matched on promise + mechanism fields (never a seed). A closed issue, a passed expiry, a hit that no longer occurs, a duplicate, or a lookup that cannot prove the issue open fails the run. Offline (`--issue-state offline [--issue-state-file F]`, the default) skips the closed-issue check with a loud warning.

## P8 surfaces_agree (test-only)

Occupant first: the person reads the Status card, chart, briefing and Next User Action line to learn what the house is held to. If the thermostat is armed 64-72 F at night while Next User Action still reasons from 68-74 F, the page cannot be trusted. P8 asks, per surface, "does the range you quote equal the range the engine is armed with right now?".

- **Readers call the real production code** on the FakeHass coordinator session (`tools/sim_harness/surfaces.py`); nothing is re-derived. Armed reference: `coordinator.current_hold().band` (the schedule band `engine._resolved_comfort_band()`, or a time-of-use bank's band while the bank owns the thermostat, Issue #1404; see [Sim Harness Spec](sim-harness-spec.md) for the justification). Surfaces: chart (`coordinator._chart_display_band()`, the band the chart draws, + `_target_band_lower_upper_now()`, fed a synthetic hourly forecast through its own `hourly_forecast_override` argument), the real `ClimateAdvisorStatusView.get()` (`ca_target_heat/cool`), the real `generate_briefing()` HVAC Mode row, `_compute_next_action()` boundary-probed (it exposes no band, so indoor is swept and the guidance flip points are compared), `engine._ceiling_threshold()`, `_nat_vent_target_now()` (must sit inside the band), the live thermostat setpoint (must not be looser than the armed edge), and the status and fan cards (`_compute_automation_status`, `_compute_next_automation_action`, `_compute_fan_status`, `_compute_whf_status`, `_compute_hvac_fan_status`, which must answer and agree with the physical fan). **`chart_strips` (Issue #1436):** the chart's future activity, both sources it draws from (the real `timeline_payload()["strips"]` and the real `get_chart_data()["predicted_activity"]`), must follow the PLAN, not the armed band: both directions (a strip where the plan has nothing, and a plan item with no strip), sampled on the 30-minute grid plus 30 s either side of every span end, step time and run/burst bound, with exact Win plan endpoints: Win plan where a (not vetoed) window period holds; Nat-vent where a plan session (start/stop steps) runs inside one, `fan_active` only inside one; Fan where such a session or the plan's own fan steps run; Heating/Cooling where a predicted run's placed minutes fall (`PredictedRun.bursts`), adding up to its expected minutes within 1 min; Away from the setback step to midnight (Away) or the end of the plan (Vacation); Override until the resume step (`tools/sim_harness/strip_check.py`; sampled on every plan change and at most every 2 virtual hours). Before the fix 85 of 135 goldens diverged here (predicted_activity's windows/fan hours against the plan); 0 after.
- **Applicability gates (documented, not tuned to pass):** chart only on a steady stretch (the hourly bucket equals both neighbours) with no held arrival restore, and only for a household config that carries the four required sleep keys (the config flow guarantees them; the engine and chart use different built-in defaults when they are absent); briefing only awake on a heat/cool day; thermostat hold only in a quiet state (no override, grace, pause, session or recent CA command), only when the live value is what CA last wrote, and only after it persists longer than the welcome-home debounce plus 5 min; a looser-than-armed hold is a miss, a tighter one (pre-cool, TOU banking) is deliberate.
- **Harness shim:** scenario `occupancy_*` events drive the engine only, so the coordinator's `_occupancy_mode` is aligned to the engine's for the duration of one sample (restored after), reproducing production, where the coordinator changes mode first.
- **Not in `PROMISES`:** that tuple holds pure `check(PromiseInput, PromiseConfig)` functions that also run at home; P8 needs live objects. `P8_KEY`, `P8_SEVERITY="hard"`, `P8_HOME_LEVEL=None` live in `surfaces.py`.
- **Ratchet:** `tools/simulations/surface_divergences.json` lists divergences known on HEAD (`surface`, `scenario` or `context: generated`, `issue`, `evidence`). Unlisted divergence fails; a listed row that no longer diverges also fails. Goldens are LOCKED, so nothing is waived by editing one.
- **Run it:** `python tools/simulate.py --surfaces [--generate N --seeds S ...]` (all 91 goldens plus optional generated days); unit tier: `tests/test_promise_surfaces.py` (goldens plus the fixed generated sample seeds 101/102 x 10 days); nightly: `.github/workflows/nightly.yml` (P8 over fresh seeds).

## Promotion to alert policy

1. `promise_stats[key]` tracks `hits`, `first_ts`, `last_ts`; "two quiet weeks" means about 14 days without a hit on the live install.
2. Every hit seen meanwhile is fixed (issue plus regression test) or its `PromiseNumbers` value is tuned with a note. Never silently waived.
3. Promotion is a one-line `home_level` change in `PROMISES`, shipped as a normal patch release (version bump, `add_fix_entry.py`, CHANGELOG). The design names P1, P3, P4 and P5 as candidates; P5b, P6, P7 and P10 stay log. Changing `PROMISES` needs human sign-off.
